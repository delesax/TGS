## Flows to processors

- **Anthropic (AI):** the text of handles and Dispatches (with the Campaign question and type) for the safety check;
  the draft text for the optional tone check and coach. Never name, email or profile. See `PROCESSORS.md`.
- **Resend (email):** recipient address, subject and body of the emails in rows 17 and 18.
- **Supabase (database, auth, email for sign-up confirmation):** everything stored.
- **Vercel (hosting):** requests to the site; no database content is stored there.
