# Online Safety Act 2023: the owner's working record

The Grand Strategist is a **user-to-user service** with UK users (people post content that other users can
read). This is a working record for the owner and the solicitor. **It does not claim compliance.** Anything not
checked is **[OWNER TO CONFIRM]**. Check Ofcom's current guidance and duties for services of this size before
launch **[OWNER TO CONFIRM]**.

## (a) Illegal-content risk assessment, in plain terms

| Kind of illegal content a debate site could host | Likelihood here | How bad | Controls |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hate speech and incitement to hatred | Medium (debates on contested topics) | High | Community rules; AI screening holds for a person; Report button; removal; appeal |
| Threats, harassment, stalking | Medium | High | Same; mute and close accounts for repeat breaches |
| Terrorism content, child sexual abuse material | Very low (invite-only adults, text only, no uploads or images) | Very high | No image or file upload exists; text screening; report route; **[OWNER TO CONFIRM the reporting steps to the police and NCA / IWF]** |
| Encouraging or assisting suicide or serious self-harm | Low to medium (assisted dying Campaign is planned, wave 4) | Very high | Not opened until conditions in `OWNER_DECISIONS.md`; content note with support lines; stricter AI thresholds; held by default when AI is down; second reviewer on call |
| Fraud, scams, spam | Low | Medium | Invite-only; spam rule; rate limits; reports |
| Defamation (illegal in the civil sense, handled by notice) | Medium | Medium | Terms: how to report; removal; author contacted |
| Sharing others' private information | Low to medium | High | Rules; reports; removal |
| Content that is illegal and revealed only by context | Low | Varies | Human review |

Controls common to all: **reporting** on every Dispatch, **human review** queue, **holds** (never silent
deletion), **removal** by a person with a stated reason, **appeal** to a person, an **audit record** of each
decision, **account muting and closure**. Review the assessment at least yearly and before any new feature
(images, files, direct messages, open sign-up) **[OWNER TO CONFIRM review dates]**.

## (b) Children's access

Access is limited by **invite-only sign-up** and by an **18+ rule** (a tick at sign-up, recorded with the time).
Ofcom's guidance treats a self-declared tick-box as **weak age assurance**. Today that is mitigated because every
account needs a personal invite from a named person, but the invite does not itself prove age.

**[OWNER AND SOLICITOR TO DECIDE before opening beyond named invitees]:** whether stronger age assurance is
needed, and what.

## (c) Reporting and complaints routes, and who handles them

| Route | What it is | Who handles it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Report button on a Dispatch | Reason and note, one per person per Dispatch | Admins, in Admin → Moderation |
| Appeal a hold, removal or decision | From the notice; once | A person (admin) decides |
| Appeal a Verdict | From the Verdict page | An admin, with the second reviewer when in place |
| "Contact us about your data" | Privacy requests and complaints | Admin → Privacy requests; reply in one month |
| Email to the contact address | Reports of unlawful content, with Dispatch, reason, name and email | **[OWNER TO CONFIRM mailbox and who reads it]** |

With one admin, appeals are decided by the same person who may have made the original decision; the queue warns
and the audit entry records it. A second reviewer is required before the first sensitive Campaign.

## (d) What the Terms say

The Terms of use say how to report illegal content (the "Reporting a Dispatch" section), that we may hold, hide
or remove content and close accounts, and how appeals work ("Moderation, removal and appeals"). They are
written in plain language **[OWNER AND SOLICITOR TO CONFIRM they are clear enough for the duty]**.
