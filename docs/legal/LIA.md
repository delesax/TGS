# Legitimate interests assessments (LIA)

Draft for the solicitor, 7 November 2026. Three-part test for each: purpose, necessity, balancing.
**[OWNER TO CONFIRM]** each before relying on it.

## 1. Moderation, including AI checks

- **Purpose:** keep debates safe and respectful: block hate, harassment and impersonation, protect people from
  self-harm content, apply the community rules consistently. Also the Online Safety Act duties of a user-to-user
  service.
- **Necessity:** a small team cannot read every Dispatch quickly. The AI check lets a person read only what is
  held. Less intrusive routes (reports only) leave harmful content up longer. The AI sees only the text and the
  Campaign question and type.
- **Balancing:** people expect some checking on a public debate site and are told in the privacy policy, the AI
  page and at the point of use. Safeguards: hold never delete; human review of every hold and appeal; reasons
  stored; no decision with legal or similar effect by AI alone; decisions kept 12 months; right to object and to
  ask a person to look again. Risk of harm from a wrong hold is limited by the appeal. **Conclusion:** the
  interest outweighs the impact. The person can object; we consider each objection.

## 2. Security logs

- **Purpose:** detect abuse and attacks; protect the service and people's data.
- **Necessity:** providers keep ordinary logs (IP address, requests) by default and short-term; we add no
  tracking of our own. Rate limits and invite checks need such data.
- **Balancing:** low intrusion, short retention set by the providers **[OWNER TO CONFIRM the periods]**, expected
  by anyone using a website. **Conclusion:** outweighs.

## 3. The safety and accountability record (`audit_log`)

- **Purpose:** show who did what and why (moderation decisions, appeals, account actions, privacy-request
  handling), to answer complaints and defend or bring legal claims, and to keep admins accountable.
- **Necessity:** a record that cannot be quietly changed is the point; it uses reference numbers rather than
  names. Without it we could not show a fair process.
- **Balancing:** the record is only readable by admins, holds minimal personal data, and expires after six years
  (the usual period for contract and negligence claims) **[OWNER TO CONFIRM with the solicitor]**. Known
  weakness: older entries may contain a handle (the code has been fixed to stop this, but existing rows cannot
  be edited). **Conclusion:** outweighs, provided the handle point is disclosed (it is, in the delete page).
